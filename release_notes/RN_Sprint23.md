# Release Note Sprint 23 (August 1 – August 17, 2026)

## New Features & Improvements

- Launched the new PrismaX marketing website at prismax.ai, showcasing Robotics Data and VLA Foundry — with plan details, contributor earning opportunities, and direct access to the data console and operator portal.
- Launched the PrismaX community forum where users can share experiences, ask questions, and connect with others.
- Users can now hold both Operator and QA reviewer roles simultaneously, allowing more flexible participation in the platform.
- Optimized loading performance across the platform, including a smoother episode upload flow and faster robotics data detail view.
- Improved Upload SDK reliability and efficiency for a more stable episode submission experience.
